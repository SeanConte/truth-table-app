Interactive Truth Table Builder

How to use: Add statements to compare. Click a cell to see its dependencies (expands subformulas + shows highlighting). Click again to cycle through blank → T → F. Main statement columns are lightly shaded.
Colors: Purple (pulsing) = selected cell/connective, Red = left operand, Green = right operand, Yellow = scope.
